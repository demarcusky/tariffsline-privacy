Privacy policy

Tariff Line · Last updated 2026-10-04

Tariff Line does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

What runs where

  • All cost calculations run locally in your browser.
  • The extension reads the title and price of the supplier page you are viewing only to calculate your result. That content is never sent anywhere.
  • Settings and saved items are stored in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and are never uploaded.

Network requests

The extension makes requests only to public customs-tariff services, and sends no user data with them:

  • hts.usitc.gov — US duty rates (USITC Harmonized Tariff Schedule).
  • trade-tariff.service.gov.uk — UK duty rates (HMRC Trade Tariff).

Australian and EU duty rates are included with the extension, so no request is made for those markets.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is none to share.
