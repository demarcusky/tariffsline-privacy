Tariff Line

See a supplier listing's landed cost and margin in-page — duty, freight and VAT for the US, UK, Australia and the EU.

Tariff Line is a browser extension for sellers. It reads the supplier page you are already viewing, suggests a customs (HS) code, looks up the current duty rate, and shows the landed cost per unit and your margin. All calculations run in your browser.
